# Quality Checklist

- Did I identify the research question or objective?
- Did I separate method, findings, and interpretation?
- Did I state the unique contribution clearly?
- Did I include critical points, not just strengths?
- Did I avoid inventing missing details?
- Did I mark uncertainty when text was incomplete or OCR-noisy?
- Did I include page numbers for direct quotes?
- Did I keep quotes short and useful?
- Did I support comparison claims with the source text?
